# Assigned Security Responsibility Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-002
**HIPAA Citation:** §164.308(a)(2) — Assigned Security Responsibility
**Status:** ADOPTED
**Owner:** [ROLE — Security Official]
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy designates the individuals responsible for the development, implementation, and ongoing operation of the security and privacy safeguards protecting the electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) handled by FHIRBridge, satisfying the §164.308(a)(2) requirement to identify a single Security Official, and the parallel Privacy Rule requirement to identify a Privacy Official.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Organization's security and privacy governance for all FHIRBridge environments and workforce members.

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Security Official Designation (§164.308(a)(2)) — REQUIRED
- The Organization designates a single **Security Official** who is responsible for developing and implementing the policies and procedures required by the HIPAA Security Rule for FHIRBridge.
- **Designated Security Official:** [ORGANIZATION TO COMPLETE — name] serving as [ROLE — Security Official].
- The Security Official's responsibilities include, without limitation:
  - Owning this policy manual and its review cycle.
  - Leading risk analysis and risk management (POL-001).
  - Overseeing information system activity review and audit-control operation (POL-001, POL-010).
  - Coordinating security incident response and breach determination (POL-006).
  - Overseeing workforce security, access management, and training (POL-003, POL-004, POL-005).
  - Serving as the primary point of contact for security matters with customers, auditors, and regulators.

### 3.2 Privacy Official Designation — REQUIRED (Privacy Rule)
- The Organization designates a **Privacy Official** responsible for the development and implementation of privacy policies and procedures and for handling privacy complaints.
- **Designated Privacy Official:** [ORGANIZATION TO COMPLETE — name] serving as [ROLE — Privacy Official]. The Security Official and Privacy Official may be the same individual where organizational size makes that appropriate. [ORGANIZATION TO COMPLETE — confirm whether combined or separate.]

### 3.3 Authority and Resources
- The designated officials are granted the authority and resources necessary to carry out their responsibilities, including budget, tooling, and access to leadership.
- The designations remain in effect until formally reassigned in writing. On any change, the Revision History table below is updated and the change communicated to the workforce within [ORGANIZATION TO COMPLETE — e.g., 5 business days].

### 3.4 Delegation
- The officials may delegate operational tasks (e.g., log review, access provisioning) but retain accountability. Delegations are documented.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| [ROLE — Security Official] | Overall accountability for the HIPAA Security Rule program for FHIRBridge. |
| [ROLE — Privacy Official] | Accountability for privacy policies, complaints, and permitted uses/disclosures. |
| [ROLE — Executive Sponsor] | Confirms designations; ensures officials have authority and resources. |

## 5. Enforcement / Sanctions

Failure of a designated official to perform assigned duties, or of any workforce member to cooperate with the officials, is subject to the sanction policy in POL-001 §3.3.

## 6. Review Cadence

Reviewed at least **annually** and upon any personnel change affecting the designations.
