# Business Associate Agreement (BAA) — Template

> **DISCLAIMER — READ FIRST.** This document is a **template** provided to help structure a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by qualified legal counsel**. Laws, regulations, and organizational circumstances vary; state law (including breach-notification law) may impose additional or stricter requirements. **Do not sign or rely on this document without having it reviewed and adapted by your attorney.** Bracketed fields such as `[PARTY NAME]` and `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` must be completed for each specific agreement. Delete this disclaimer only from the executed copy at counsel's direction.

## Business Associate Agreement

This Business Associate Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of **[EFFECTIVE DATE]** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

- **[COVERED ENTITY / UPSTREAM PARTY NAME]**, a **[STATE/ENTITY TYPE]**, having its principal place of business at **[ADDRESS]** ("**Covered Entity**" or, where this Agreement is used downstream, the "**Business Associate/Upstream Party**"); and
- **[BUSINESS ASSOCIATE / DOWNSTREAM PARTY NAME]**, a **[STATE/ENTITY TYPE]**, having its principal place of business at **[ADDRESS]** ("**Business Associate**").

Each a "**Party**" and collectively the "**Parties**."

> **Directional note (delete before signing).** Use this template in either direction. **Upstream:** the customer/health-care organization is *Covered Entity* and FHIRBridge is *Business Associate*. **Downstream/flow-down:** FHIRBridge stands in the *Covered Entity* role and the subprocessor is *Business Associate*. The subprocessor's obligations must be at least as protective as those FHIRBridge owes upstream.

### Recitals

WHEREAS, the Parties have entered into, or will enter into, one or more agreements under which Business Associate provides services to or on behalf of Covered Entity (the "**Underlying Services Agreement**"); and

WHEREAS, in performing those services Business Associate may create, receive, maintain, or transmit Protected Health Information ("**PHI**"), including electronic PHI ("**ePHI**"), on behalf of Covered Entity; and

WHEREAS, the Parties intend to comply with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and its implementing regulations, as amended by the HITECH Act and the Omnibus Rule (collectively, "**HIPAA**"), including the Privacy Rule (45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164, Subparts A and E) and the Security Rule (Subpart C);

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows.

---

### 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in HIPAA (45 CFR §§ 160.103 and 164.501), including but not limited to: **Breach**, **Business Associate**, **Covered Entity**, **Data Aggregation**, **Designated Record Set**, **Disclosure**, **Electronic Protected Health Information**, **Health Care Operations**, **Individual**, **Minimum Necessary**, **Protected Health Information**, **Required By Law**, **Secretary** (of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services), **Security Incident**, **Subcontractor**, **Unsecured Protected Health Information**, and **Use**.

For purposes of this Agreement, "**PHI**" means Protected Health Information that Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits for or on behalf of Covered Entity under the Underlying Services Agreement.

---

### 2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures of PHI

2.1 **General limitation.** Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the services set out in the Underlying Services Agreement, as permitted or required by this Agreement, or as Required By Law. Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in any manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as set forth in Sections 2.2–2.3.

2.2 **Management, administration, and legal responsibilities.** Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities. Business Associate may disclose PHI for those purposes only if (a) the disclosure is Required By Law, or (b) Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient that the PHI will be held confidentially and used or further disclosed only as Required By Law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and the recipient notifies Business Associate of any breach of confidentiality.

2.3 **Data aggregation and de-identification.** Business Associate may (a) provide Data Aggregation services relating to the Health Care Operations of Covered Entity, and (b) de-identify PHI in accordance with 45 CFR § 164.514(a)–(c), **only** if and to the extent expressly authorized in the Underlying Services Agreement or in Exhibit A.

2.4 **Minimum Necessary.** Business Associate shall, in its Use and Disclosure of PHI and in any request for PHI, limit PHI to the **minimum necessary** to accomplish the intended purpose of the Use, Disclosure, or request, consistent with 45 CFR § 164.502(b) and § 164.514(d).

2.5 **Prohibited uses.** Business Associate shall not sell PHI or use or disclose PHI for marketing or fundraising except as permitted by HIPAA and expressly authorized in writing by Covered Entity.

---

### 3. Obligations of Business Associate

Business Associate agrees to:

3.1 **Not use or disclose** PHI other than as permitted or required by this Agreement or as Required By Law.

3.2 **Safeguards.** Use appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, and comply with the Security Rule (45 CFR §§ 164.308, 164.310, 164.312, and 164.316) with respect to ePHI, to prevent Use or Disclosure of PHI other than as provided for by this Agreement. This includes implementing and maintaining reasonable and appropriate safeguards such as encryption of ePHI in transit and at rest, access controls, unique user identification, audit logging, and integrity controls.

3.3 **Mitigation.** Mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect that is known to Business Associate of a Use or Disclosure of PHI in violation of this Agreement.

3.4 **Reporting.** Report to Covered Entity, in accordance with **Section 4**, any Use or Disclosure of PHI not permitted by this Agreement, any Security Incident, and any Breach of Unsecured PHI of which it becomes aware.

3.5 **Subcontractor flow-down.** In accordance with 45 CFR §§ 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees **in writing** to restrictions and conditions **at least as restrictive** as those that apply to Business Associate under this Agreement, including the Security Rule requirements with respect to ePHI. Business Associate remains responsible for its Subcontractors' compliance.

3.6 **Access to PHI (§164.524).** Within **[NUMBER, e.g., 10]** business days of a request by Covered Entity, make available PHI in a Designated Record Set to Covered Entity (or, as directed, to the Individual) as necessary to satisfy Covered Entity's obligations under 45 CFR § 164.524, including provision of an electronic copy where the PHI is maintained electronically.

3.7 **Amendment of PHI (§164.526).** Within **[NUMBER, e.g., 10]** business days of a request by Covered Entity, make any amendment(s) to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed or agreed to by Covered Entity pursuant to 45 CFR § 164.526.

3.8 **Accounting of disclosures (§164.528).** Document and maintain records of Disclosures of PHI and information related to such Disclosures as would be required for Covered Entity to respond to a request for an accounting of disclosures under 45 CFR § 164.528, and provide such information to Covered Entity (or the Individual) within **[NUMBER, e.g., 30]** days of a request.

3.9 **Availability to the Secretary.** Make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the Use and Disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary for purposes of determining compliance with HIPAA.

3.10 **Compliance with Covered Entity's obligations.** To the extent Business Associate is to carry out one or more of Covered Entity's obligations under Subpart E of Part 164, comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in the performance of such obligation(s).

3.11 **Notice of privacy practices / restrictions.** Comply with any limitations in Covered Entity's Notice of Privacy Practices, and with any restriction on the Use or Disclosure of PHI that Covered Entity has agreed to or is required to abide by under 45 CFR § 164.522, to the extent such limitations or restrictions are communicated to Business Associate.

---

### 4. Reporting of Security Incidents and Breaches

4.1 **Impermissible use/disclosure and Security Incidents.** Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any Use or Disclosure of PHI not permitted by this Agreement and any Security Incident of which it becomes aware, **without unreasonable delay** and in no event later than **[NUMBER, e.g., 5]** business days after discovery. The Parties acknowledge that unsuccessful Security Incidents (e.g., routine pings, port scans, or failed log-in attempts that result in no unauthorized access to, or Use or Disclosure of, ePHI) are hereby reported on an aggregate basis to the extent required by law, and no separate notice is required for each such event.

4.2 **Breach of Unsecured PHI — §164.410 timeline.** Following discovery of a **Breach** of Unsecured PHI, Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity **without unreasonable delay and in no case later than sixty (60) calendar days** after discovery of the Breach, as required by 45 CFR § 164.410. The Parties may agree in writing to a shorter notice period (recommended: **[NUMBER, e.g., 10]** calendar days) to allow Covered Entity to meet its own § 164.404 obligations. A Breach is treated as **discovered** as of the first day on which it is known, or by exercising reasonable diligence would have been known, to Business Associate.

4.3 **Contents of a Breach notice.** To the extent known, the Breach notification shall include: (a) the identification of each Individual whose Unsecured PHI has been, or is reasonably believed to have been, accessed, acquired, used, or disclosed; (b) a description of what happened, including date of the Breach and date of discovery; (c) the types of PHI involved; (d) steps Individuals should take to protect themselves; (e) steps Business Associate is taking to investigate, mitigate harm, and prevent recurrence; and (f) contact information. Business Associate shall supplement the notice with additional information as it becomes available.

4.4 **Cooperation.** Business Associate shall cooperate with Covered Entity in Covered Entity's investigation, notification, and mitigation efforts, and shall preserve all relevant records.

---

### 5. Obligations of Covered Entity

5.1 Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any limitation(s) in its Notice of Privacy Practices, of any changes in or revocation of an Individual's permission to Use or Disclose PHI, and of any restriction on Use or Disclosure of PHI to which Covered Entity has agreed under 45 CFR § 164.522, to the extent such matters may affect Business Associate's Use or Disclosure of PHI.

5.2 Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to Use or Disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under Subpart E of Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted under Sections 2.2–2.3.

---

### 6. Term and Termination

6.1 **Term.** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and remains in effect until all PHI provided by, or created or received by Business Associate on behalf of, Covered Entity is returned or destroyed in accordance with Section 6.4, or until terminated as provided herein.

6.2 **Termination for breach (cause).** If Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has materially breached this Agreement, Covered Entity may: (a) provide Business Associate written notice of the breach and an opportunity to cure within **[NUMBER, e.g., 30]** days, and terminate this Agreement and the Underlying Services Agreement if Business Associate fails to cure; or (b) immediately terminate if cure is not possible; or (c) if neither termination nor cure is feasible, report the violation to the Secretary.

6.3 **Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement as provided in the Underlying Services Agreement or upon **[NUMBER]** days' prior written notice, subject to Section 6.4.

6.4 **Return or destruction of PHI upon termination.** Upon termination or expiration, Business Associate shall, if feasible, **return or destroy all PHI** received from, or created, maintained, or received by Business Associate on behalf of, Covered Entity, and shall retain no copies (including with respect to Subcontractors). If return or destruction is **not feasible**, Business Associate shall (a) extend the protections of this Agreement to such PHI, (b) limit further Uses and Disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for as long as Business Associate maintains the PHI, and (c) provide Covered Entity a written statement identifying the PHI retained and the reason return/destruction is infeasible. Destruction shall be performed in a manner rendering the PHI unreadable, indecipherable, and unrecoverable (e.g., NIST SP 800-88 media sanitization; cryptographic erasure of encrypted data). Business Associate shall certify destruction in writing upon request.

6.5 **Survival.** The obligations of Business Associate under this Section 6 and any provision that by its nature should survive termination shall survive.

### 7. Miscellaneous

7.1 **Regulatory references.** A reference to a section of HIPAA means the section as in effect or as amended, and for which compliance is required.

7.2 **Amendment.** The Parties agree to amend this Agreement as necessary for the Parties to comply with the requirements of HIPAA and other applicable law. No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

7.3 **Interpretation.** Any ambiguity in this Agreement shall be resolved to permit the Parties to comply with HIPAA. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and the Underlying Services Agreement regarding PHI, this Agreement controls.

7.4 **No third-party beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Agreement confers any rights on any person other than the Parties, except as expressly required by HIPAA.

7.5 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **[STATE]**, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles, except to the extent preempted by federal law.

7.6 **Indemnification / liability.** **[TO BE NEGOTIATED — indemnification, limitation of liability, and insurance (including cyber liability) provisions per counsel; consider whether these live here or in the Underlying Services Agreement.]**

7.7 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the contacts in Exhibit B (or as later updated in writing).

7.8 **Counterparts / electronic signature.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
