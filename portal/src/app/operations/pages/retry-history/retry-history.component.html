Retry History

Every retry attempt against a transient pipeline or webhook failure.

What am I looking at?

Every row is one automatic retry attempt — the system hit a brief, transient problem (a short network blip, a momentarily unreachable destination) and tried the same operation again with a short delay, instead of giving up immediately.

Is this something to worry about?

A retry succeeding on a later attempt is normal and healthy — it means FHIRBridge absorbed a brief hiccup so you didn't have to. Repeated retries against the same destination or connection are the pattern worth watching — that usually points to a persistent issue (an unstable connection, a destination that's frequently unavailable) rather than one-off noise.

If retries are exhausted and the operation still fails, it shows up separately in the Errors tab — this screen only records the retry attempts themselves.
