Endpoint Health

Periodic connectivity checks against enabled source connections and most file/cloud-storage destinations. SQL Server/database destinations and SFTP aren't monitored yet.

What am I looking at?

Every row is one periodic connectivity check — a lightweight "can we still reach it" ping, not an actual data sync.

What's covered

Every enabled source connection, plus most file-export and cloud-storage destinations (S3, Blob Storage, Snowflake, CSV/Excel exports, and similar). SQL Server/database destinations and SFTP aren't monitored yet.

This screen shows nothing until an administrator turns on connectivity monitoring — an empty table usually means it isn't enabled yet, not that everything is offline.
