Validation Failures

Resources that produced US-Core / data-quality warnings during normalization — structural, never clinical content.

What am I looking at?

Every row means: a resource came back from the source EHR itself missing something required — before your field mapping ever touches it. This is not about your mapping configuration being wrong; it's the raw data the EHR sent us. Example: Epic returns a Patient with no gender populated — that shows up here as a warning, with a Data Quality Score reflecting how incomplete the record is.

Why it matters: without this, the only way you'd learn about incomplete source data is a customer noticing missing fields downstream, weeks later, with no way to trace when it started or whether it's your issue or the EHR's. This screen is the paper trail — when someone later asks "why is this patient's data incomplete," you can point to the exact resource, the exact missing field, and the exact time it happened.

This screen being empty is a good sign — it means your source data is passing all quality checks, not that nothing was checked. A Data Quality Score is included per resource when available, so you can spot resources that are borderline even without an outright warning.

Only structural details (which field, which resource type) are shown — never the actual clinical content of the record.
