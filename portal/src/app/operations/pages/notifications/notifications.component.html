What am I looking at?

Every row is one outbound notification Segue sent — today, that's either an export-delivery email (a destination configured to deliver its file by email) or an alert-rule email (a threshold you configured was breached). Email is the only notification channel today.

If a notification shows Failed, check your SMTP/email settings and the recipient address first — that's the most common cause, and something you can fix without contacting support.
