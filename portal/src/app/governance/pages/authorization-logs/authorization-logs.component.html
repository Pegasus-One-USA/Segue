Authorization Logs

RBAC permission denials — a user attempted an action their role does not grant.

What am I looking at?

Every row is one action a logged-in user was blocked from doing because their role doesn't grant that permission — not a login problem, a permissions problem. The user was successfully identified; they just weren't allowed to do that specific thing.

What to do about it

If a user is being denied something they should legitimately be able to do, that's a role/permission configuration fix in User Management — not something wrong with this log. Frequent denials for the same user/action can also indicate a misunderstanding of what their role covers.
