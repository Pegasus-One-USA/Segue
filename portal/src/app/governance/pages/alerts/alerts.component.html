Alerts

Real alert-rule firings — each row is an actual evaluated condition that reached its threshold, with a real email sent to its recipients.

What am I looking at?

Every row is one time an Alert Rule you configured actually fired — a background job checked its condition on a schedule, found it was met, and emailed the configured recipients. This is history of firings, not the rules themselves — configure those under Alert Rules.

Rules can currently only be built against Security Events (lockouts, secret rotations, etc.) — not against other log types like Errors or Pipeline Executions.
