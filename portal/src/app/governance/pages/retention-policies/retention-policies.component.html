Retention Policies

The real, currently-effective retention period for every governance and operations log type. The four HIPAA audit tables are never purged.

What am I looking at?

This isn't a settings form — it's a read-only view of what's actually enforced right now. A background job periodically deletes rows older than the shown retention period for each log type marked "Purgeable" — this genuinely happens automatically, it's not just a policy on paper.

A handful of tables (Audit Logs, Authentication Logs, SMART Launch Logs, Data Access Logs) are marked "immutable" and never purged — these are the tables HIPAA requires to be kept as permanent evidence.
