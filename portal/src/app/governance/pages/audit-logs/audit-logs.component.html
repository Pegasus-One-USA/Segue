Audit Logs

Immutable, hash-chained record of every configuration and entity change — who, what, when.

What am I looking at?

Every row is one configuration or record change — creating, editing, or deleting a source connection, destination, mapping profile, user, role, or any other governed setting. It's tamper-evident: each row is cryptographically chained to the one before it, so a row can never be quietly altered or deleted without breaking the chain.

Reading a row

Actor is who made the change. Entity shows the display name of the record that was changed — not who changed it. If Actor shows anonymous, that usually means a self-service action taken before login (like a password reset) — not a suspicious unauthenticated change.

Click "View diff" on a row to see exactly which fields changed, old value vs. new value.
