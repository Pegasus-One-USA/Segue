Data Access Logs

Who accessed which patient/resource, when, and why — PHI-free by design (identifiers only).

What am I looking at?

Every row is one governance decision about a specific patient/resource record — allowed or denied, during a pipeline run. It records identifiers only (patient ID, resource type) — never the actual clinical content of the record.

Coverage note

This is populated by the classic Pipeline Route engine. Data moved through the newer Workflow-canvas engine is not currently recorded here — if you're relying on this screen for a complete access audit, confirm which engine your data flows use.
